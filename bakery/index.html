Why sourdough?

drag me →

🎧

The crackle

That noise when the crust gives. Genuinely the best part of the week.

🍋

The tang

Wild starter, slow ferment, that sour little kick at the back of every bite.

🫧

The crumb

Big irregular holes. Butter falls straight through and we think that's romantic.

💪

The chew

Real structure. It fights back a little, in a way that soft bread never does.

🏠

The smell

Carry one home and the whole place smells like a bakery for an hour.

⏳

The wait

Nothing about sourdough is fast, which is exactly why it tastes like this.

Once it's home

Keep it
happy.

These are drafts — tell me how you actually want people to store it ✎

1

Cut side down

Stand it on the board with the cut face flat. No bag, no box, no cling film for the first day.

2

Paper, not plastic

After day one, a paper bag or a clean tea towel. Plastic softens the crust you paid for.

3

Freeze in slices

Slice first, freeze flat, toast straight from frozen. Tastes like day one for weeks.
